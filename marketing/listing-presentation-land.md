### SLIDE 4: Why Land Marketing is Different

"Selling land is fundamentally different from selling a house. With a house, buyers can walk in and picture themselves living there. With land, they have to imagine everything. Our job is to help them see the potential."

**The challenges specific to land:**
- No interior to photograph
- Buyers can't visualize the buildable area, views, or access from a listing description
- Boundary lines, topography, and features like creeks or springs are invisible in standard photos
- Cell phone photos of trees and dirt all look the same

"That's why our marketing approach for land goes way beyond what a typical agent does."

---

"For land listings, drone photography isn't a nice-to-have. It's essential. It's the only way to truly show what a buyer is getting."

**What our drone package includes:**

### SLIDE 9: Property Information Package

"For every land listing, I assemble a complete property information package that answers the questions buyers are going to ask before they even ask them."

**The package includes:**
- Zoning and restriction status (unrestricted vs. HOA/deed restricted)
- County setback and building requirements
- Soil test/perc test results (if available)
- Well and septic feasibility
- Available utilities (power, water, internet at the road)
- Road maintenance agreement (if private road)
- Flood zone status (FEMA map)
- Property tax history and current assessment
- Timber value estimate (for heavily wooded parcels, if applicable)
- Any existing surveys, easements, or right-of-ways

"Most land listings have none of this information readily available. Buyers have to call the agent and wait for answers, or worse, the agent doesn't know. We put it all together upfront so buyers can make informed decisions quickly."

### SLIDE 14: Pricing Your Land

"Now let's talk about pricing. Land is harder to price than a house because every parcel is unique. There's no cookie-cutter formula."

**Factors that affect land value in WNC:**
- Acreage and usable/buildable area
- Road access (paved vs. gravel vs. 4WD only)
- Utility availability at the lot line
- Water features (creek, spring, river frontage)
- Views (long-range mountain views command premiums)
- Restrictions (unrestricted land typically sells for more)
- Soil test/septic approval status
- Elevation and sun exposure
- Proximity to town
- Timber value

**The data on pricing right:**
- After **14 days on market**, listing views dramatically decrease
  *(Source: Zillow / Redfin market data)*

- Homes (and land) priced right go under contract in a median of **5 days**, while properties requiring a price reduction take a median of **35 days total**
  *(Source: HomeLight study)*
