# Weekly Video/Post Marketing Plan

## 9-Week Content Calendar Using Video Hooks

---

### Week 1: "Before you buy a house this year, watch this"
**Format:** 60-sec video or carousel post
**Content:** 3 things that changed in 2026 for WNC buyers. Cover interest rate reality, inventory levels being up 41% since Helene, and why waiting for "the perfect time" costs more than people think. End with "DM me if you're thinking about buying in Western NC."
**Post on:** Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn, GBP

### Week 2: "3 things buyers regret after closing"
**Format:** 60-sec video
**Content:** Not getting a well flow test, skipping the septic inspection, and not checking road maintenance agreements. All specific to mountain properties. These are real issues you've seen. End with "I walk my buyers through all of this before we even make an offer."
**Post on:** Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn, GBP

### Week 3: "If you're waiting for interest rates to drop... watch this first"
**Format:** 60-sec video
**Content:** Rates may drop but prices won't. Use actual WNC median price data. "When rates dropped slightly last fall, inventory got absorbed in weeks." The math on buying now vs waiting. Keep it factual, not pushy.
**Post on:** Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn, GBP

### Week 4: "One thing sellers are doing that's hurting their home sale"
**Format:** 60-sec video
**Content:** Overpricing by 10-15% and sitting on market for 80+ days. WNC days on market is averaging 69-80 right now. Homes priced right are selling. Homes priced on emotion are expiring. Offer to do a free CMA for anyone thinking about listing.
**Post on:** Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn, GBP

### Week 5: "No one tells you this about moving to Waynesville"
**Format:** 90-sec video
**Content:** The real stuff. Gravel roads, propane heat, cell service gaps, elevation differences within the same town, flood insurance surprises. All factual, no steering, no neighborhood ratings. Link to your Waynesville blog post. Repeat this hook for a different town each quarter.
**Post on:** Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn, GBP

### Week 6: "A real estate tip I wish more buyers knew"
**Format:** 60-sec video or text post with photo
**Content:** Unrestricted vs restricted land in WNC and why it matters more here than anywhere else. Most buyers from out of state have no idea what "unrestricted" means or why it affects value. Link to your unrestricted land blog post.
**Post on:** Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn, GBP

### Week 7: "What $500K actually buys right now"
**Format:** Carousel post (4-5 slides) or video walkthrough
**Content:** Show 3-4 real listings at different price points around $500K across your towns. A cabin in Bryson City, land with acreage in Franklin, a home in Waynesville. Let the properties speak for themselves. No "perfect for" language.
**Post on:** Facebook, Instagram, GBP

### Week 8: "The biggest mistake I see first-time buyers make"
**Format:** 60-sec video
**Content:** Not getting pre-approved before touring. In WNC's competitive pockets, sellers won't even look at an offer without one. Walk through what pre-approval actually involves and why it takes 5 minutes with a good lender. Offer to connect people with a local lender.
**Post on:** Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn, GBP

### Week 9: "The market changed this month and buyers need to know this"
**Format:** 60-sec video or text post with chart
**Content:** Pull fresh data from Canopy Realtors press releases. Cover median price, inventory, days on market for your counties. Keep it numbers-only, let people draw their own conclusions. This becomes your recurring monthly market update post.
**Post on:** Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn, GBP
