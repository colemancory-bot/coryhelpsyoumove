Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 3, 2026

Introduction

This privacy policy explains how Cory Coleman, licensed real estate agent with Keller Williams Great Smokies ("we," "us," or "our"), collects, uses, and protects your personal information when you visit coryhelpsyoumove.com (the "Site") or use our real estate services.

Information We Collect

Information You Provide

  • Account registration: First name, last name, email address, and phone number (optional) when you create an account via email, Google, or Facebook sign-in
  • Consultation requests: Name, email, phone, interest type, and message when you submit the consultation form
  • Chat conversations: Messages you send through the on-site chatbot assistant

Information Collected Automatically

  • Property activity: Properties you view, save as favorites, or search for
  • Preferences: Your theme preference (dark/light mode)
  • Authentication session: Login state to keep you signed in between visits
  • Usage analytics: Your IP address, browser and device type, the pages you view, and how you reached the Site, collected automatically by Google Analytics

How We Use Your Information

  • To provide real estate services and connect you with listing information
  • To respond to your inquiries and consultation requests
  • To save your property favorites and search preferences
  • To send property alerts and market updates you have opted into
  • To improve the Site and your browsing experience

Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services to operate the Site:

  • Supabase: Authentication and secure database storage (hosted in the United States)
  • Google OAuth: Optional sign-in via your Google account. Google's privacy policy applies to data shared during sign-in.
  • Facebook Login: Optional sign-in via your Facebook account. Meta's privacy policy applies to data shared during sign-in.
  • Follow-Up Boss: Customer relationship management (CRM) to help us serve you better and follow up on your real estate inquiries
  • GitHub Pages: Website hosting
  • Google Analytics: Aggregate website usage analytics (page views, traffic sources, device and browser type) that help us improve the Site. Google may set cookies and process your IP address and device information under its own privacy policy.
  • Resend: Email delivery for the notifications and alerts you request
  • Google Fonts: Web fonts loaded from Google, which may receive your IP address when the Site loads

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to any third party for marketing purposes.

Cookies & Local Storage

This Site uses browser local storage (similar to cookies) to remember your preferences:

  • cc-theme: Your dark/light mode preference
  • cc-supabase-auth: Authentication session to keep you signed in
  • cc_profile: Cached profile information for faster page loads
  • _ga and _ga_*: Google Analytics cookies that measure aggregate Site usage

We use Google Analytics to understand how visitors use the Site. It sets its own cookies and may collect your IP address and device information, which Google processes under its privacy policy. We do not serve ads on this Site, run advertising or retargeting campaigns, or use third-party advertising cookies. You can opt out of Google Analytics with Google's opt-out browser add-on.

Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Correct any inaccurate information
  • Delete your account and all associated data (see our Data Deletion page)
  • Opt out of marketing communications at any time

Data Retention

We retain your personal information for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide you with our services. If you request deletion of your account, we will remove your data within 30 days. See our Data Deletion page for instructions.

Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your personal information, including encrypted data transmission (HTTPS), secure authentication, and access controls on our database. However, no method of transmission over the Internet is 100% secure.

Children's Privacy

This Site is not directed at individuals under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date. Your continued use of the Site after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
