About

Cory Coleman, Sylva NC Real Estate Agent

Keller Williams Great Smokies · NC Real Estate License #351304 · Serving Jackson, Haywood, Macon, and Swain counties · (828) 506-6413

Cory Coleman is a licensed North Carolina real estate agent with Keller Williams Great Smokies, based at the brokerage's office at 96 W Sylva Shopping Area in Sylva. He works with buyers and sellers across the four westernmost counties that most people mean when they say "the WNC mountains": Jackson, Haywood, Macon, and Swain.

That covers a specific set of towns he drives every week: Sylva, Waynesville, Maggie Valley, Bryson City, Franklin, Cashiers and Highlands, Dillsboro, and Cullowhee. Mountain property here comes with questions that flatland real estate never asks: wells and septic systems, gravel access roads, slope, elevation, flood zones on creek lots, and county rules that change at every county line.

How Cory works

The short version: research first, pressure never. This site is part of that. The county-by-county short-term rental guide, the property tax breakdown, and the relocation guide exist because buyers and sellers deserve real answers before anyone talks about a transaction. If you want a data-backed read on your own home, the home valuation tool is free and comes with an honest follow-up, not a sales script.
